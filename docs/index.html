Credit Committee Memorandum

Risk Rating: 3 - Moderate / Watch

Executive Summary: Ashanti Harvest Grain Aggregators Ltd. requests a $120,000.00 revolving seasonal commodity working capital line at 11.5% for 36 months to fund direct harvest-gate procurement of maize. While the borrower exhibits strong operational backing with 11 years of management experience and signed forward off-take agreements covering 70% of volume, the facility generates a pro-forma DSCR of 1.19x, breaching the policy floor of >= 1.35x. Furthermore, the current ratio sits at a WATCH level of 1.27x against a 1.30x threshold, justifying a conditional approval structure backed by strict warehouse receipt collateral and receivables assignments.

Financial Performance: Over the FY24 to FY26 period, revenue expanded from $780,000.00 to $945,000.00 in FY25, and reached $1,120,000.00 in FY26. However, gross margins compressed by 200 basis points from 21.0% in FY24 (gross profit $163,800.00) to 20.0% in FY25 ($189,000.00) and 19.0% in FY26 ($212,800.00) due to farm-gate price competition and transport fuel inflation. EBITDA grew correspondingly from $95,800.00 in FY24 to $111,500.00 in FY25 and $124,800.00 in FY26, translating to stable EBITDA margins of 12.28%, 11.8%, and 11.14%. Liquidity remains tight, with the current ratio declining from 1.39 in FY24 to 1.35 in FY25, and resting at 1.27 in FY26. Leverage remains manageable with total debt increasing from $120,000.00 to $137,000.00, resulting in debt-to-EBITDA improving from 1.25x down to 1.1x, though pro-forma total debt expands to $257,000.00 and pro-forma debt-to-EBITDA rises to 2.06x.

Debt Service & Stress: Historical DSCR stood at 2.32x in FY24, 2.38x in FY25, and 2.50x in FY26, supported by existing debt service of $36,500.00, $40,800.00, and $43,200.00 respectively. With the addition of the new annual debt service of $47,485.45, the pro-forma total debt service reaches $90,685.45, generating a baseline pro-forma DSCR of 1.19x against a policy floor of 1.35x. A 250 bps rate shock yields a rate-shocked DSCR of 1.17x. Multi-variable stress testing demonstrates vulnerability to combined shocks; minor COGS increases such as Rev +0% / COGS +3.0% reduce DSCR to 0.89x, and severe shocks result in multiple sub-1.0x scenarios, requiring robust operational monitoring.

Risks & Structural Mitigants

  • Risk: Pro-Forma DSCR of 1.19x breaches the policy minimum threshold of >= 1.35x.
    Mitigant: Strict collateral coverage via 1.35x warehouse receipts on graded maize and direct assignment of Tier-1 poultry off-taker receivables.
  • Risk: Current ratio of 1.27x falls below the policy threshold of >= 1.30x.
    Mitigant: Implementation of a mandatory cash sweep and ongoing working capital monitoring covenants.
  • Risk: Gross margin compression of 200 bps down to 19.0% driven by farm-gate price competition and fuel inflation.
    Mitigant: Signed 12-month forward off-take agreements covering 70% of projected volume lock in sales pricing.
  • Risk: Concentration risk where top two off-takers account for 58% of accounts receivable and AR days extend to 37.5.
    Mitigant: Direct remittance alignment and escrowed settlement accounts tied to the assigned off-taker receivables.
  • Risk: Post-harvest moisture and weevil spoilage risks during storage (inventory days at 63.6).
    Mitigant: Mandatory adherence to warehouse aeration protocols, moisture-tested intake below 13%, and third-party warehouse receipt monitoring.

Conditions Precedent & Covenants

  1. Execution and perfection of warehouse receipt collateral on graded white and yellow maize achieving at least 1.35x coverage.
  2. Legal assignment and direct notice of acknowledgment executed with the top two poultry off-takers for receivables collection.
  3. Establishment of a lockbox or controlled collection account for all off-taker settlements to sweep directly against the revolving facility.
  4. Ongoing financial covenant compliance: Maintain Pro-Forma Debt / EBITDA <= 3.00x, Interest Coverage >= 2.25x, and EBITDA Margin >= 8.5%.
  5. Monthly submission of inventory aging reports, warehouse receipt valuations, and accounts receivable aging schedules to verify current ratio stabilization above 1.27x.

Credit Committee Memorandum

Risk Rating: 2 - Satisfactory

Executive Summary: Charter Oak Companion & Home Care LLC requests a $150,000.00 revolving working capital line of credit at 8.75% for 48 months to bridge bi-weekly payroll ahead of state waiver and private-pay reimbursements. The credit presents a strong risk profile with a pro-forma DSCR of 2.39x and pro-forma Debt/EBITDA of 1.07x, comfortably passing all policy covenant thresholds. All covenants, including a Current Ratio of 1.70x and Interest Coverage of 18.08x, are fully compliant, supporting an approval recommendation under a Satisfactory risk rating.

Financial Performance: The borrower has demonstrated robust multi-year trajectory, with revenue growing from $840,000.0 in 2024 to $1,060,000.0 in 2025 and $1,310,000.0 in 2026, driven by expanding Connecticut Home Care Program for Elders referrals and private-pay clients. Gross margins expanded from 37.0% in 2024 to 38.0% in 2025 and 39.0% in 2026, while EBITDA margins increased from 12.83% ($107,800.0) to 14.65% ($155,300.0) and peaked at 16.4% ($214,900.0) in 2026 due to automated electronic visit verification scheduling and overtime reduction. Liquidity remains sound with the current ratio strengthening from 1.57x in 2024 to 1.64x in 2025 and 1.70x in 2026. Leverage improved steadily, with total debt-to-EBITDA decreasing from 0.77x in 2024 to 0.53x in 2025 and 0.38x in 2026.

Debt Service & Stress: Historical DSCR strengthened from 3.41x in 2024 to 4.45x in 2025 and 5.62x in 2026. The new annual debt service of $44,579.71 brings the pro-forma total debt to $231,000.0 and pro-forma total debt service to $77,579.71, resulting in a healthy pro-forma DSCR of 2.39x. A 200 bps rate shock results in a rate-shocked DSCR of 2.34x, demonstrating strong resilience. Stress testing indicates that the borrower maintains a DSCR above the 1.25x policy threshold under moderate shocks, while sub-1.00x DSCR scenarios are strictly confined to severe combined revenue declines of 15% to 20% and cost escalations.

Risks & Structural Mitigants

  • Risk: Accounts receivable days increased from 42.6 days in 2024 to 43.5 days in 2026 due to state Medicaid waiver reimbursement batching cycles.
    Mitigant: First-lien UCC-1 filing on $145,000.0 eligible accounts receivable with regular aging monitoring and a conservative advance rate.
  • Risk: Direct care workforce turnover requires continuous recruiting and onboarding spend.
    Mitigant: Robust historical EBITDA generation ($214,900.0 in 2026) and seasoned management with 14 years of combined healthcare administration and financial operations experience.

Conditions Precedent & Covenants

  1. Execution of standard loan documentation including a 48-month revolving line of credit agreement at 8.75% interest.
  2. Perfected first-priority UCC-1 lien filing covering all accounts receivable ($145,000.0 eligible A/R) and blanket business assets.
  3. Receipt of unconditional personal guarantees from all managing principals.
  4. Ongoing compliance with a minimum Pro-Forma DSCR of >= 1.25x, maximum Pro-Forma Debt/EBITDA of <= 3.50x, minimum Current Ratio of >= 1.20x, minimum Interest Coverage of >= 2.00x, and minimum EBITDA Margin of >= 10.0%.

Credit Committee Memorandum

Risk Rating: 1 - Low Risk

Executive Summary: We recommend the approval of a $175,000.00 term loan for Elm City Auto & Fleet Services LLC to finance diagnostic equipment and fleet inventory. The borrower demonstrates strong financial performance with a 2026 EBITDA of $252,400.00 and a robust pro-forma DSCR of 2.62x. All financial covenants comfortably pass current policy thresholds, supported by first-lien equipment collateral and personal guarantees.

Financial Performance: Over the three-year historical period from 2024 to 2026, Elm City Auto & Fleet Services LLC has exhibited strong top-line growth, with revenue expanding from $920,000.00 in 2024 to $1,085,000.00 in 2025, and reaching $1,240,000.00 by fiscal year 2026. Gross margins improved steadily from 52.0% in 2024 to 53.0% in 2025 and 53.5% in 2026, driven by parts-supplier volume rebates. Profitability also strengthened, with EBITDA increasing from $157,400.00 (17.11% margin) in 2024 to $204,550.00 (18.85% margin) in 2025, and $252,400.00 (20.35% margin) in 2026. Liquidity remains stable with the current ratio hovering near 1.43 in 2026, while leverage has decreased significantly as total debt dropped from $180,000.00 in 2024 to $120,000.00 in 2026, pushing debt-to-EBITDA down to 0.48.

Debt Service & Stress: The facility requires a new annual debt service of $42,832.13, bringing pro-forma total debt to $295,000.00 and pro-forma total debt service to $84,632.13. This results in a solid pro-forma DSCR of 2.62x and a pro-forma debt-to-EBITDA of 1.17x, both well within policy thresholds. A 200 bps interest rate shock yields a rate-shocked DSCR of 2.55x, demonstrating high resilience to interest rate fluctuations. Multi-variable stress testing indicates that the borrower maintains a DSCR above 1.0x under moderate downturn scenarios, dropping below 1.0x only under severe combined shocks such as a 20% revenue drop alongside a 5% or higher COGS increase.

Risks & Structural Mitigants

  • Risk: Accounts receivable days stretched from 24.6 days in 2024 to 27.7 days in 2026 due to 45-day net billing cycles on municipal fleet accounts.
    Mitigant: Municipal and delivery-van fleet contracts provide highly stable, recurring revenue comprising 42% of total sales, counterbalancing the slower collection cycles.
  • Risk: Certified EV and diagnostic technician retention remains competitive across southern Connecticut.
    Mitigant: The managing member brings 18 years of ASE Master Technician experience and maintains strong operational leadership with a clean personal credit score of 745 FICO.

Conditions Precedent & Covenants

  1. Execution and delivery of mutually acceptable loan documentation, including a first-lien UCC-1 filing on diagnostic lifts and equipment valued at $140,000.00, blanket business assets, and personal guarantees.
  2. Verification of good standing with the State of Connecticut.
  3. Maintenance of a minimum Pro-Forma DSCR of >= 1.25x tested annually.
  4. Maintenance of a maximum Pro-Forma Debt / EBITDA of <= 3.50x.
  5. Maintenance of a minimum Current Ratio of >= 1.20x.
  6. Maintenance of a minimum Interest Coverage ratio of >= 2.00x.
  7. Maintenance of a minimum EBITDA Margin of >= 10.0%.
