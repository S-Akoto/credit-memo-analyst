Credit Committee Memorandum

Risk Rating: 3 - Moderate / Watch

Executive Summary: We recommend conditional approval of a $120,000.00 revolving seasonal commodity working capital line for Ashanti Harvest Grain Aggregators Ltd. at 11.5% interest over 36 months. While the borrower demonstrates steady revenue expansion to $1,120,000.00 in FY2026 and robust interest coverage at 5.87x, the facility yields a pro-forma DSCR of 1.19x, breaching the policy threshold of >= 1.35x. Furthermore, the current ratio sits at 1.27x, falling slightly below the 1.30x policy floor, necessitating strict collateral monitoring and operational covenants.

Financial Performance: Over the three-year historical period from FY2024 to FY2026, Ashanti Harvest demonstrated consistent top-line growth, with revenue increasing from $780,000.00 to $945,000.00 in FY2025, and reaching $1,120,000.00 in FY2026. Gross profit expanded from $163,800.00 (21.0% margin) in FY2024 to $189,000.00 (20.0% margin) in FY2025, and $212,800.00 (19.0% margin) in FY2026, showing a 200 bps compression due to farm-gate price competition and transport fuel inflation. EBITDA followed a similar upward trajectory, rising from $95,800.00 (12.28% margin) in FY2024 to $111,500.00 (11.8% margin) in FY2025, and $124,800.00 (11.14% margin) in FY2026. Liquidity remains tight as the current ratio contracted from 1.39 in FY2024 to 1.35 in FY2025 and 1.27 in FY2026. Leverage improved moderately as debt-to-EBITDA moved from 1.25x in FY2024 down to 1.13x in FY2025 and 1.1x in FY2026, though the new facility elevates pro-forma total debt to $257,000.00 and pro-forma debt-to-EBITDA to 2.06x.

Debt Service & Stress: Existing annual debt service is $43,200.00 as of FY2026, supported by a historical DSCR of 2.5x and interest coverage of 5.87x. The new facility introduces $47,485.45 in annual debt service, bringing pro-forma total debt service to $90,685.45 and resulting in a tight pro-forma DSCR of 1.19x. A 250 bps interest rate shock reduces the rate-shocked DSCR marginally to 1.17x. However, multi-variable stress testing reveals significant vulnerability to margin compression: combination shocks such as 0% revenue change with a 3.0% COGS increase drop the DSCR to 0.89x, and a 12.0% COGS spike drops it to -0.01x, indicating that strict gross margin management is vital to debt service protection.

Risks & Structural Mitigants

  • Risk: Pro-Forma DSCR of 1.19x breaches the policy minimum threshold of >= 1.35x.
    Mitigant: Robust historical interest coverage of 5.87x and strict assignment of poultry off-taker receivables.
  • Risk: Current ratio of 1.27x falls below the policy floor of >= 1.30x due to tight working capital liquidity.
    Mitigant: Warehouse receipt collateral providing 1.35x coverage on graded white/yellow maize inventory.
  • Risk: Gross margin compression of 200 bps down to 19.0% driven by farm-gate price competition and fuel inflation.
    Mitigant: Signed 12-month forward off-take agreements covering 70% of projected volume with Tier-1 poultry producers.
  • Risk: Concentration risk where the top two off-takers account for 58% of accounts receivable.
    Mitigant: Direct farm-gate procurement from over 420 smallholder farmers eliminates middleman leakage, while direct collection accounts secure cash flows.
  • Risk: Post-harvest moisture and weevil spoilage risk threatening stored grain collateral value.
    Mitigant: Mandatory adherence to moisture testing (<13%) and rigorous warehouse aeration protocols.

Conditions Precedent & Covenants

  1. Execution and registration of first-lien warehouse receipts on graded white and yellow maize providing minimum 1.35x collateral coverage.
  2. Legal assignment of accounts receivable from the top two poultry off-takers.
  3. Establishment of a controlled collection account (lockbox/escrow mechanism) for off-taker settlements.
  4. Implementation of monthly inventory audits verifying grain quality, moisture content below 13%, and active aeration protocols.
  5. Borrower submission of quarterly financial statements and compliance certificates demonstrating adherence to a minimum Current Ratio of 1.25x and maintenance of Pro-Forma Debt / EBITDA below 3.00x.

Credit Committee Memorandum

Risk Rating: 1 - Low Risk

Executive Summary: We recommend the approval of a $175,000.00 term loan for Elm City Auto & Fleet Services LLC to fund equipment acquisitions and working capital at an 8.25% interest rate over 60 months. The borrower demonstrates strong financial performance with 2026 revenue of $1,240,000.00, an EBITDA of $252,400.00, and a healthy pro-forma DSCR of 2.62x. All credit policy covenants pass comfortably, supported by solid collateral including a first-lien UCC-1 on equipment valued at $140,000.00 and blanket business assets.

Financial Performance: Elm City Auto & Fleet Services LLC has shown consistent top-line growth, with revenue increasing from $920,000.00 in 2024 to $1,085,000.00 in 2025, and reaching $1,240,000.00 in 2026. Gross margins expanded from 52.0% in 2024 to 53.5% in 2026 due to direct parts-supplier volume rebates. EBITDA margins strengthened from 17.11% ($157,400.00) in 2024 to 20.35% ($252,400.00) in 2026. Liquidity remains stable with a current ratio of 1.43 in 2026, while leverage has dropped significantly, with total debt to EBITDA falling from 1.14 in 2024 to 0.48 in 2026.

Debt Service & Stress: The transaction adds a new annual debt service of $42,832.13, bringing total pro-forma debt service to $84,632.13 against a pro-forma total debt of $295,000.00. The resulting pro-forma DSCR is 2.62x, easily exceeding the policy threshold of >= 1.25x. A rate shock of 200 bps yields a resilient rate-shocked DSCR of 2.55x. Stress testing indicates the facility maintains adequate buffer under moderate shocks, though combined revenue declines of -15% or -20% alongside COGS increases push the DSCR below 1.0x.

Risks & Structural Mitigants

  • Risk: Accounts receivable days stretched from 24.6 days in 2024 to 27.7 days in 2026 due to 45-day net billing cycles on municipal fleet accounts.
    Mitigant: Municipal contracts represent stable, high-credit recurring revenue (42% of total revenue) with negligible default risk.
  • Risk: Certified EV and diagnostic technician retention is competitive across southern Connecticut.
    Mitigant: The managing member brings 18 years of ASE Master Technician and operational experience with a clean 745 FICO score, ensuring strong operational leadership.

Conditions Precedent & Covenants

  1. Execution and delivery of first-lien UCC-1 filing on diagnostic lifts and equipment valued at $140,000.00.
  2. Receipt of blanket business assets security agreement and personal guarantees from the principal.
  3. Maintenance of Pro-Forma DSCR at or above 1.25x tested annually.
  4. Maintenance of Current Ratio at or above 1.20x tested annually.
