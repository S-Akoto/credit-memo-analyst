### Financial Performance Commentary
Over the FY24 to FY26 period, revenue expanded from $780,000.00 to $945,000.00 in FY25, and reached $1,120,000.00 in FY26. However, gross margins compressed by 200 basis points from 21.0% in FY24 (gross profit $163,800.00) to 20.0% in FY25 ($189,000.00) and 19.0% in FY26 ($212,800.00) due to farm-gate price competition and transport fuel inflation. EBITDA grew correspondingly from $95,800.00 in FY24 to $111,500.00 in FY25 and $124,800.00 in FY26, translating to stable EBITDA margins of 12.28%, 11.8%, and 11.14%. Liquidity remains tight, with the current ratio declining from 1.39 in FY24 to 1.35 in FY25, and resting at 1.27 in FY26. Leverage remains manageable with total debt increasing from $120,000.00 to $137,000.00, resulting in debt-to-EBITDA improving from 1.25x down to 1.1x, though pro-forma total debt expands to $257,000.00 and pro-forma debt-to-EBITDA rises to 2.06x.

## 5. Debt Service & Sensitivity Stress Analysis
- **Base Pro-Forma DSCR:** 1.19x
- **Rate-Shocked DSCR (+250 bps):** 1.17x

Historical DSCR stood at 2.32x in FY24, 2.38x in FY25, and 2.50x in FY26, supported by existing debt service of $36,500.00, $40,800.00, and $43,200.00 respectively. With the addition of the new annual debt service of $47,485.45, the pro-forma total debt service reaches $90,685.45, generating a baseline pro-forma DSCR of 1.19x against a policy floor of 1.35x. A 250 bps rate shock yields a rate-shocked DSCR of 1.17x. Multi-variable stress testing demonstrates vulnerability to combined shocks; minor COGS increases such as Rev +0% / COGS +3.0% reduce DSCR to 0.89x, and severe shocks result in multiple sub-1.0x scenarios, requiring robust operational monitoring.

## 6. Key Credit Risks & Structural Mitigants
- **Risk:** Pro-Forma DSCR of 1.19x breaches the policy minimum threshold of >= 1.35x.
  - **Mitigant:** Strict collateral coverage via 1.35x warehouse receipts on graded maize and direct assignment of Tier-1 poultry off-taker receivables.
- **Risk:** Current ratio of 1.27x falls below the policy threshold of >= 1.30x.
  - **Mitigant:** Implementation of a mandatory cash sweep and ongoing working capital monitoring covenants.
- **Risk:** Gross margin compression of 200 bps down to 19.0% driven by farm-gate price competition and fuel inflation.
  - **Mitigant:** Signed 12-month forward off-take agreements covering 70% of projected volume lock in sales pricing.
- **Risk:** Concentration risk where top two off-takers account for 58% of accounts receivable and AR days extend to 37.5.
  - **Mitigant:** Direct remittance alignment and escrowed settlement accounts tied to the assigned off-taker receivables.
- **Risk:** Post-harvest moisture and weevil spoilage risks during storage (inventory days at 63.6).
  - **Mitigant:** Mandatory adherence to warehouse aeration protocols, moisture-tested intake below 13%, and third-party warehouse receipt monitoring.

## 7. Conditions Precedent & Ongoing Covenants
1. Execution and perfection of warehouse receipt collateral on graded white and yellow maize achieving at least 1.35x coverage.
1. Legal assignment and direct notice of acknowledgment executed with the top two poultry off-takers for receivables collection.
1. Establishment of a lockbox or controlled collection account for all off-taker settlements to sweep directly against the revolving facility.
1. Ongoing financial covenant compliance: Maintain Pro-Forma Debt / EBITDA <= 3.00x, Interest Coverage >= 2.25x, and EBITDA Margin >= 8.5%.
1. Monthly submission of inventory aging reports, warehouse receipt valuations, and accounts receivable aging schedules to verify current ratio stabilization above 1.27x.
