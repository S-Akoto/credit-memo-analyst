## 2. Executive Recommendation
We recommend conditional approval of a $120,000.00 revolving seasonal commodity working capital line for Ashanti Harvest Grain Aggregators Ltd. at 11.5% interest over 36 months. While the borrower demonstrates steady revenue expansion to $1,120,000.00 in FY2026 and robust interest coverage at 5.87x, the facility yields a pro-forma DSCR of 1.19x, breaching the policy threshold of >= 1.35x. Furthermore, the current ratio sits at 1.27x, falling slightly below the 1.30x policy floor, necessitating strict collateral monitoring and operational covenants.

### Financial Performance Commentary
Over the three-year historical period from FY2024 to FY2026, Ashanti Harvest demonstrated consistent top-line growth, with revenue increasing from $780,000.00 to $945,000.00 in FY2025, and reaching $1,120,000.00 in FY2026. Gross profit expanded from $163,800.00 (21.0% margin) in FY2024 to $189,000.00 (20.0% margin) in FY2025, and $212,800.00 (19.0% margin) in FY2026, showing a 200 bps compression due to farm-gate price competition and transport fuel inflation. EBITDA followed a similar upward trajectory, rising from $95,800.00 (12.28% margin) in FY2024 to $111,500.00 (11.8% margin) in FY2025, and $124,800.00 (11.14% margin) in FY2026. Liquidity remains tight as the current ratio contracted from 1.39 in FY2024 to 1.35 in FY2025 and 1.27 in FY2026. Leverage improved moderately as debt-to-EBITDA moved from 1.25x in FY2024 down to 1.13x in FY2025 and 1.1x in FY2026, though the new facility elevates pro-forma total debt to $257,000.00 and pro-forma debt-to-EBITDA to 2.06x.

## 5. Debt Service & Sensitivity Stress Analysis
- **Base Pro-Forma DSCR:** 1.19x
- **Rate-Shocked DSCR (+250 bps):** 1.17x

Existing annual debt service is $43,200.00 as of FY2026, supported by a historical DSCR of 2.5x and interest coverage of 5.87x. The new facility introduces $47,485.45 in annual debt service, bringing pro-forma total debt service to $90,685.45 and resulting in a tight pro-forma DSCR of 1.19x. A 250 bps interest rate shock reduces the rate-shocked DSCR marginally to 1.17x. However, multi-variable stress testing reveals significant vulnerability to margin compression: combination shocks such as 0% revenue change with a 3.0% COGS increase drop the DSCR to 0.89x, and a 12.0% COGS spike drops it to -0.01x, indicating that strict gross margin management is vital to debt service protection.

## 6. Key Credit Risks & Structural Mitigants
- **Risk:** Pro-Forma DSCR of 1.19x breaches the policy minimum threshold of >= 1.35x.
  - **Mitigant:** Robust historical interest coverage of 5.87x and strict assignment of poultry off-taker receivables.
- **Risk:** Current ratio of 1.27x falls below the policy floor of >= 1.30x due to tight working capital liquidity.
  - **Mitigant:** Warehouse receipt collateral providing 1.35x coverage on graded white/yellow maize inventory.
- **Risk:** Gross margin compression of 200 bps down to 19.0% driven by farm-gate price competition and fuel inflation.
  - **Mitigant:** Signed 12-month forward off-take agreements covering 70% of projected volume with Tier-1 poultry producers.
- **Risk:** Concentration risk where the top two off-takers account for 58% of accounts receivable.
  - **Mitigant:** Direct farm-gate procurement from over 420 smallholder farmers eliminates middleman leakage, while direct collection accounts secure cash flows.
- **Risk:** Post-harvest moisture and weevil spoilage risk threatening stored grain collateral value.
  - **Mitigant:** Mandatory adherence to moisture testing (<13%) and rigorous warehouse aeration protocols.

## 7. Conditions Precedent & Ongoing Covenants
1. Execution and registration of first-lien warehouse receipts on graded white and yellow maize providing minimum 1.35x collateral coverage.
1. Legal assignment of accounts receivable from the top two poultry off-takers.
1. Establishment of a controlled collection account (lockbox/escrow mechanism) for off-taker settlements.
1. Implementation of monthly inventory audits verifying grain quality, moisture content below 13%, and active aeration protocols.
1. Borrower submission of quarterly financial statements and compliance certificates demonstrating adherence to a minimum Current Ratio of 1.25x and maintenance of Pro-Forma Debt / EBITDA below 3.00x.
