### Financial Performance Commentary
Over the three-year historical period from 2024 to 2026, Elm City Auto & Fleet Services LLC has exhibited strong top-line growth, with revenue expanding from $920,000.00 in 2024 to $1,085,000.00 in 2025, and reaching $1,240,000.00 by fiscal year 2026. Gross margins improved steadily from 52.0% in 2024 to 53.0% in 2025 and 53.5% in 2026, driven by parts-supplier volume rebates. Profitability also strengthened, with EBITDA increasing from $157,400.00 (17.11% margin) in 2024 to $204,550.00 (18.85% margin) in 2025, and $252,400.00 (20.35% margin) in 2026. Liquidity remains stable with the current ratio hovering near 1.43 in 2026, while leverage has decreased significantly as total debt dropped from $180,000.00 in 2024 to $120,000.00 in 2026, pushing debt-to-EBITDA down to 0.48.
